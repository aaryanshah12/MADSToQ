MADSToQ Services

IT services & custom SaaS from Ahmedabad.

MADSToQ is an IT services provider in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We design custom software and SaaS platforms for teams that still run critical work in spreadsheets, registers, and disconnected tools.

From the first process map to training and support, we stay with the people who use the system every day — chemists, store teams, accounts, sales, and owners.

What We Offer

End-to-end IT services in Ahmedabad: discovery, custom software, SaaS rollout, and the support that keeps a live system accurate.

Companies come to MADSToQ when stock, material movement, costing, or sales tracking has outgrown WhatsApp threads and Excel. We turn that work into software people can actually run — with clear roles, an audit trail, and screens that match the job on the floor.

Requirement Discovery & Process Mapping

We sit with the people who enter the data and the people who sign off on it. Together we map how material, money, and approvals actually move — including the exceptions that never make it into a requirements document. The output is a practical plan: what to build first, what to leave manual, and how you will know the system is working.

Custom Software Development

When a ready product is not the right fit, we design and build the workflow ourselves. That includes dashboards, entry screens, approvals, reports, and the small checks that stop a wrong quantity or a missing reference from slipping through. The interface stays quiet. The record underneath stays complete.

SaaS Products You Can Start On

Not every engagement starts from a blank page. Inventory covers stock, usage, and role-based reporting. Inward-Outward replaces manual material registers with traceable movement and quotations. PMC turns price references and recipes into product RMC. CRM runs plotting schemes, plots, leads, bookings, and collections. We implement these platforms, then extend them where your process is different.

Deployment, Training & Continuous Support

Go-live is a working week, not a handover email. We train each role on the screens they own, watch the first real entries, and fix the friction while it is still small. After that we stay available for enhancements — new reports, a new approval, a field the business did not need on day one.

How an engagement usually runs

Most projects in Ahmedabad and across Gujarat start with one painful process, not a full digital transformation programme. We scope that process, agree what “correct” looks like, and ship a version the team can use. Parallel work — a second plant, a broker desk, a costing sheet — comes after the first workflow is trusted.

You can expect a named point of contact, written process notes, and software that your operators can explain back to us. If you are comparing an IT SaaS or IT services provider in Ahmedabad, look at whether the vendor will still be in the room when the first month of real data arrives. That is the part MADSToQ keeps.
